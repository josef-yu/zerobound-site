Privacy Policy — Zerobound

Last updated: 27 August 2026

In short: Zerobound collects nothing. The app has no internet access, requests no permissions, and does not gather, transmit, or sell any personal data.

This policy explains how the mobile game Zerobound (package com.ontex.zerobound), developed by Ontex, handles information. It applies to the app distributed through Google Play.

Data we collect

None. Zerobound does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The app is shipped without the Internet permission, so it cannot send information anywhere.

On-device diagnostics

To help improve the game during testing, Zerobound may keep a local diagnostics log on your device only. This log records gameplay events such as puzzle states, timings, and a coarse device type (for example, the phone model class) — never your name, email, contacts, location, or any account or advertising identifier. Nothing in this log leaves your device automatically.

During the current test (alpha) release, you may choose to export this log and send it to the developer to report a problem. That is entirely manual and optional; if you never export it, the developer never receives it.

Advertising

The current version contains no ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play "Data safety" details will be updated before that version is released.

Platform data (Google Play & Android)

Zerobound is distributed via Google Play and runs on Android. Google and your device's operating system may collect certain information independently of the app — for example, installation statistics, and crash or performance diagnostics (Android Vitals) — under Google's own policies, which we do not control. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Zerobound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact
